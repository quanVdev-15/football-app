I need you to act as a senior full-stack developer. Help me build a web app for managing my Saturday football team in Vietnam.

CONTEXT:
- 40 total players in the group
- Saturday matches only, 7vs7, 2 hours total, each match is 15 minutes
- 19-22 players typically show up, sometimes up to 28
- Players can bring guests (ringers) if numbers are low
- Payment: 35,000 VND per match OR 150,000 VND monthly subscription (unlimited matches that month)
- We have ONE trusted accountant. Everyone pays him via bank transfer or Momo/ZaloPay.
- We are friends. No strike system for no-shows. Trust and communication only.

PROBLEMS TO SOLVE:
1. Facebook polls are messy for RSVP tracking
2. With 28 players (4 teams), simple A vs B / C vs D means each team only plays once (boring)
3. No fair lineup system for 3 teams (19-22 players) or 4 teams (23-28 players)
4. Payment tracking is manual — accountant needs to know WHO paid and WHO still owes

REQUIRED FEATURES:

A) RSVP SYSTEM
- Players mark "IN" for Saturday
- Players can add "+1 guest" (guest name optional)
- Real-time count of expected players
- Cutoff time: Friday 8pm (after that, lineup is locked)

B) PAYMENT TRACKING (VIETNAM STYLE)
- Each player has: name, phone, subscription status
- Monthly subscription: 150,000 VND, valid for 30 days
- Pay-per-play: owes 35,000 VND per match they attend
- After RSVP cutoff, app shows the accountant ONE screen:
  "Today's expected players: 22"
  "Monthly members (no fee): 8 people"
  "Pay-per-play (owe 35k each): 12 people = 420,000 VND"
  "Guests (owe 35k each): 2 people = 70,000 VND"
  "TOTAL TO COLLECT: 490,000 VND"
- Accountant can check boxes next to each name as they pay (bank transfer or cash)
- No automated payment gateway — just manual confirmation by accountant

C) TEAM SCHEDULER
Given N players checked in, automatically generate a fair rotation:

IF 19-22 players (3 teams of 7, with subs):
- Round robin: Team A vs B (15 min), B vs C (15 min), C vs A (15 min)
- Each team plays 2 matches, total 45 min playing time

IF 23-28 players (4 teams of 7):
- Use rotating ladder:
  Round 1: A vs B, C vs D
  Round 2: Winners play, Losers play
  Round 3: A vs C, B vs D
  Round 4: Winners of Round 2 play each other (final)

D) LIVE "NEXT UP" BOARD
- Mobile-friendly page showing:
  "ON COURT: Team Red vs Team Blue"
  "NEXT: Team Green"
  "ON DECK: Team Yellow"
- Auto-refreshes every 30 seconds

E) GUEST HANDLING
- Guests are automatically pay-per-play (35k)
- Guest payment is tracked under the host player's name

F) NO-STRIKE POLICY
- If someone RSVPs "IN" but doesn't show, the app does NOT penalize them
- But the accountant can see the history (just for awareness)
- We handle it like adults: talk to each other

TECH SPECS:
- Build as a Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Use Firebase or Supabase (free tier is fine for 40 people)
- Mobile-first design
- No complex login — just select name from dropdown or type name
- Vietnamese language preferred but English is fine

DELIVERABLES:
1. Complete database schema
2. Full HTML/CSS/JS frontend code
3. Firebase/Firestore security rules
4. Deployment instructions for Netlify or Vercel (free)
5. Admin panel for the accountant to mark payments
